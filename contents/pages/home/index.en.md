Long term, I want to build intelligent systems that can **reason, optimize, and help us make better decisions about complex real-world problems**. I am especially interested in problems with real-world impact in **healthcare, the environment, and industry**.

A few questions keep me curious:

- **Learning + optimization:** How can machine learning help optimization algorithms make better decisions, and vice versa?
- **Structured and responsible AI:** How can we build AI systems that reason with structure, remain transparent, and behave responsibly when deployed in the real world?
- **Real-world applications:** How can these methods help us solve problems that impact real people and communities?

Outside research, I enjoy exploring whatever catches my curiosity.
